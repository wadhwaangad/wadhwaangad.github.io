MolmoAct2 · Action Reasoning Models for Real-world Deployment

Haoquan Fang, Jiafei Duan, Donovan Clay, Sam Wang, … Angad Wadhwa, … Ali Farhadi, Dieter Fox, Ranjay Krishna

An open action reasoning model for robots that work in the real world. It pairs MolmoER, a vision-language backbone for spatial and embodied reasoning, with a flow-matching action expert, and adds OpenFAST, an open action tokenizer, plus MolmoThink, a variant that adapts how deeply it reasons. It ships with three new datasets, including 720 hours of bimanual teleoperation, and is evaluated on 7 simulated and real-world robot benchmarks and 13 embodied-reasoning benchmarks.
